And step 3, the semantic adjustment, we had started on the 21st of May — but that’s exactly where we hit our blocker: the little pause sign you see on step 3 indicated we were on hold, waiting for the AI model we couldn’t install.

So that’s where we stood at our last checkpoint. And it’s precisely this blocker on step 3 that is now resolved — I’ll show you how on the next slides.”
